# Response & Governance

## Mission

Response & Governance turns evidence-backed recommendations into controlled, authorised and auditable actions.

## Boundaries

The console owns decision authority, policy evaluation, execution oversight and rollback. It consumes operational and investigative context without duplicating their full workspaces.

## Pages

Response Inbox, Action Center, Decision Register, Playbooks, Runs & Rollback, Policy Gates, Approvals & Authorities, Agent Fleet, Audit Trail and Response Metrics.
