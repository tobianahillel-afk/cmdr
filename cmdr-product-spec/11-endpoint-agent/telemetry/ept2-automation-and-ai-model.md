# Endpoint EPT-2 — Automation and AI Model

Essential functions are deterministic/manual-capable. AI may summarize volume, explain source-backed gaps/degradation and suggest a normalization issue or anomaly candidate.

AI may not invent an event/source/process/file/network activity/platform support, declare maliciousness, create Evidence/Finding, hide a gap/missing sensor, change permission/policy, or manufacture capability availability.

| Function | Deterministic path | AI role | Prohibited |
|---|---|---|---|
| normalization/quality | required | explain | invent mappings/events |
| gap/degradation | source-backed assessment | summarize | hide/erase gap |
| capability availability | dependency/state rules | explain reason | invent support |
| privacy/masking | policy rules | none required | reveal restricted data |
