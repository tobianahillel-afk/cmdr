# Open Gaps

## Product decisions

- Final launch-page set and whether lower-frequency administrative pages need additional splits.
- Canonical query language for Event Search.
- Confidence calibration model for hypotheses, findings and threat intelligence.
- Executive/MSSP aggregate metrics allowed across tenants.
- Formal closure criteria for incidents and cases.

## Security and governance

- Electronic signature and external timestamping requirements.
- Step-up authentication policy for critical decisions.
- Retention periods, legal-hold jurisdictions and export formats.
- Emergency authority and break-glass governance.
- Compliance baselines targeted for launch.

## Platform selections

- Search backend, policy engine and playbook/orchestration language.
- Required first-party versus integrated forensic engines.
- Supported deployment models and agent topologies.
- SLO, RTO and RPO values by service and data class.
- Initial integration and sandbox image catalogue.

## UX validation

- User testing of console identity and transitions.
- Density testing for SOC L1/L3, DFIR and managers.
- Accessible interaction model for maps, evidence boards, entity graphs and reverse-engineering canvases.
- Light-mode parity and reduced-motion review.

These gaps are intentionally explicit. They must be resolved through decisions in `00-governance/DECISION_LOG.md`, not by adding competing definitions to page documents.
