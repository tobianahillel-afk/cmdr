# Brand and Visual Decisions

## Direction

`Operational Editorial Modernism` communicates calm, authority, precision, responsibility, mastered density, editorial structure, readability and operational efficiency.

## Forbidden directions

Cyberpunk, military HUD, neon, glow, metal, chrome, excessive glassmorphism, decorative hexagons, decorative circuits, generic shields, decorative radar, decorative world maps, particles, game interfaces and systematic absolute black.

## Open proposals

Investigate, Govern and Studio palettes are not decided. Their documents must present argued proposals and remain proposed until sponsor approval.

## Typography direction

Inter, Inter Tight, IBM Plex Sans or an equivalent editorial sans direction; monospace for identifiers, hashes, queries, paths and code. Final licensing remains open.
