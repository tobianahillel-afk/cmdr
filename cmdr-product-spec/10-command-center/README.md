# Command Center

## Mission

Command Center provides the operational picture: what is happening, what matters first, who owns it, what business services are affected and which decisions or actions are pending.

## Boundaries

It coordinates incidents and operational work. It may summarise evidence, findings, decisions and runs, but deep investigation remains in Investigation Lab and authority remains in Response & Governance.
