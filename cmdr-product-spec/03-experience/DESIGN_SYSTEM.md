# CMDR Design System

## Objective

Create one recognisable product with three cognitively distinct consoles.

## Shared identity

CMDR uses consistent typography, spacing, object identifiers, status semantics and global navigation. Visual language must feel authored and operational, not like a generic “AI cyber dashboard”.

## Console identities

- **Command Center:** high-contrast operational instrument; teal/cyan accent, structured grids, live status and restrained motion.
- **Investigation Lab:** analytical workspace; purple/ink accent, evidence-board and notebook cues without sacrificing readability.
- **Response & Governance:** authority and accountability; gold/amber accent, decision-document structure, seals only as restrained status motifs.

Light and dark modes preserve hierarchy and semantic contrast. Console identity may not rely on colour alone.

## Status semantics

Criticality, confidence, integrity, execution and approval are separate dimensions. A single colour must never represent all of them. Text labels and icons accompany colour.

## Density

Pages are complete but not indiscriminate. Primary decisions occupy the strongest visual hierarchy. Secondary telemetry is progressively disclosed through panels, tabs, drawers or drill-downs.

## Motion

Use motion to communicate state transition, live updates or cross-console hand-off. Never animate continuously for decoration. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
