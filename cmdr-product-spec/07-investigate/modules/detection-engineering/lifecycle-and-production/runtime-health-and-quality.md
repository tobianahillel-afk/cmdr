# Runtime health and quality

CAP-INV-425 reconciles expected and observed versions. CAP-INV-426 assesses execution health. CAP-INV-427 consumes Command feedback. CAP-INV-428 reviews production matches with uncertainty.

Active, healthy, useful and effective are separate dimensions. Signal volume is not quality. Command dispositions are valuable evidence but not absolute ground truth.
