# Automation and AI

## Permitted assistance
Candidate functions and names; explanations of representations; proposed types, structures, xrefs, graphs or breakpoints; exception and trace summaries; version comparison; Hypothesis, Evidence candidate, Finding Draft and future Detection Engineering knowledge preparation.

## Deterministic and manual baseline
Disassembly and decompilation Tools; navigation; symbols; xrefs; graph and table views; comparators; manual debugger controls and breakpoints; viewers; annotations; non-agentic workflows and human review.

## Prohibitions
No silent Tool or Debugger Session, invisible breakpoint/control, silent patch, original modification, certain presentation of candidates, decompilation as original source, exception as confirmed exploit, automatic Evidence/Finding, automatic detection rule deployment, self-permission, trace removal or mandatory chatbot.

## Attribution
Initiator, producer/agent and version, Tool Calls, Automation Run, sources, parameters, timestamp, status/error, uncertainty, human owner and accept/modify/reject disposition are always visible.
