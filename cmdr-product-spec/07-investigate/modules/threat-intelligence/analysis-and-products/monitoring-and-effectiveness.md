# Monitoring and Effectiveness

CAP-INV-532 follows authorized knowledge and source changes, updates Sightings and produces Change Assessments and consumer notifications. It is not active surveillance of a target and a notification is not an Alert. CAP-INV-534 records contextual consumer feedback and effectiveness limits; CAP-INV-535 reassesses Requirement satisfaction and prepares collection feedback without executing collection.
