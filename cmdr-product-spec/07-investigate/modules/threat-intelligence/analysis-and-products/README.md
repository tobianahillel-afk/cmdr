# Threat Intelligence Analysis and Products

This submodule consumes CAP-INV-501..518 and owns CAP-INV-519..537. It covers analysis sessions, competing hypotheses, structured fusion, assessments, product planning/authoring/review, handling, internal dissemination, watchlist definitions, operationalization handoffs, monitoring, feedback, corrections and lifecycle provenance. It creates no API, exchange protocol, active watchlist, deployed Indicator, Detection rule, response action or external transmission.

## Boundary
Foundation knowledge remains versioned and sourced. Shared owns generic Report/Reporting, Entity, Graph, Timeline, Search, Linking, Versioning, Notifications, Collaboration, Export, Trace and Recovery. Command, Detection Engineering, Settings, Studio and Govern retain their canonical objects and authority.

## Capability groups
- CAP-INV-519..521 — session, hypotheses and structured fusion.
- CAP-INV-522..524 — actor, campaign and technical assessments.
- CAP-INV-525..527 — product planning, authoring and quality review.
- CAP-INV-528..533 — handling, internal dissemination, watchlists, operationalization, monitoring and external-sharing preparation.
