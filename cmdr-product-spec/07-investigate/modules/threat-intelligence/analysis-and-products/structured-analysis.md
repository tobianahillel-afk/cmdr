# Structured Analysis

CAP-INV-519 opens a bounded Analysis Session from a Handoff Package, Knowledge Project, Case, Hunt or Detection context. CAP-INV-520 keeps null, competing and dependent hypotheses visible. CAP-INV-521 records claim-level provenance, source dependency, repetition, corroboration, contradiction and missing information.

A supported hypothesis is not a fact; source agreement is not correctness; disagreement is not automatic falsity. Methods may be manual, deterministic or AI-assisted, but every output is attributed and reviewable. Tables and matrices provide a complete no-AI path.
