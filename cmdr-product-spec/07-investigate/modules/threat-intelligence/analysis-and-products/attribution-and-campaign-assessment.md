# Attribution and Campaign Assessment

CAP-INV-522 evaluates Threat Entity candidates and attribution alternatives; CAP-INV-523 evaluates Activity Cluster, Campaign and Intrusion Set groupings; CAP-INV-524 evaluates malware, tools, infrastructure and capabilities. Shared infrastructure, TTP or malware never proves a common actor. Candidate, assessment, canonical Entity and confirmed attribution remain distinct. No active infrastructure contact or offensive procedure is permitted.
