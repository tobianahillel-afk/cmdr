# Correction and Retraction

CAP-INV-536 identifies affected conclusions, sources, consumers, Indicators, watchlists and versions; it prepares correction, retraction, supersession and notifications. Correction never deletes history, retraction is not deletion, superseded is not false, and an internal notification is not an executed external recall.
