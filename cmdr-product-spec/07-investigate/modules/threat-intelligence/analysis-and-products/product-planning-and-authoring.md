# Product Planning and Authoring

CAP-INV-525 links Intelligence Requirements, audience, type, scope, sources, assessments, constraints, reviewers, markings and releasability candidate into a Product Plan. CAP-INV-526 creates a versioned Product Draft containing key judgments, sources, limitations, confidence, contradictions, alternatives, timeline, implications, non-executory recommendations, gaps and annexes.

A Product Draft is not a released product, Evidence or canonical Report. Shared Reporting owns rendering and generic report infrastructure.
