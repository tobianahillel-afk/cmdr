# Dissemination and Consumers

CAP-INV-528 prepares handling, redaction, releasability and audience rules. CAP-INV-529 permits reversible internal publication only to authorized consumers and records access, suspension, withdrawal and supersession. CAP-INV-533 prepares external-sharing evidence and an Action Request but never transmits.

Marking is not permission; releasable is not shared; internal publication is not export or external sharing; access to a product does not grant access to raw sources. OPEN-019 retains the final dissemination policy decision.
