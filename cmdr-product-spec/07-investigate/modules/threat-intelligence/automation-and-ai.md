# Automation and AI

AI may propose Requirements, source candidates, bounded extraction, candidates, aliases, relations, clusters, TTP mappings, contradictions, duplicates, confidence drafts, expiration candidates, project summaries and handoff packages.

Every essential workflow remains available through forms, deterministic parsers/extractors, catalogues, tables, filters, search, graph with table alternative, timelines, comparators, explainable rules, checklists, non-agentic workflows and human review.

Prohibited: automatic Indicator confirmation/deployment, entity/campaign/actor attribution, silent relation or duplicate merge, opaque truth score, automatic source reliability, hidden contradiction, active watchlist/rule/block, external sharing, permission grant, trace removal or mandatory chatbot.

Every automated output exposes initiator, producer/version, Automation Run, Tool Calls, sources, parameters, timestamp, status, errors, uncertainty, human owner and accept/modify/reject disposition.
