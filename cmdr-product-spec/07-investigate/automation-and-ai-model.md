# Automation and AI model — Investigate through Phase 4B.2B.2B

AI may propose candidate functions, names, types, structures, xrefs, graph interpretations, breakpoints, exception explanations, trace summaries, version comparisons, Hypotheses, Evidence candidates, Finding Drafts or future Detection Engineering knowledge.

Every essential workflow remains available through deterministic Tools, disassembly/decompilation results, navigation, symbols, xrefs, graph/table views, comparators, manual debugger controls, manual breakpoints, viewers, annotations, checklists, non-agentic workflows and human review.

Prohibitions: no silent Tool or Debugger Session; no invisible breakpoint or control; no silent patch; no original modification; no candidate as certainty; no decompilation as original source; no exception as confirmed exploit; no automatic Evidence/Finding; no automatic detection rule deployment; no trace removal; no self-permission; no mandatory chatbot; no Govern bypass.

Mandatory attribution: initiator, producer/agent and version, Tool Calls, Automation Run, sources, parameters, timestamp, status/error, uncertainty, human owner and accept/modify/reject disposition.
