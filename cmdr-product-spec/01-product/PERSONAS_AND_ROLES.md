# Personas and Roles

## SOC Analyst

Needs a clear priority queue, incident context, related signals, ownership and safe escalation. Can acknowledge, classify, assign and open investigations within scope.

## SOC Lead

Needs global posture, workload, business impact, SLA pressure, team activity and decisions awaiting attention. Can reprioritise and coordinate but does not automatically receive every high-impact response permission.

## DFIR Investigator

Needs reproducible evidence handling, timelines, entity correlation, hypotheses, findings, notes and reporting. Can verify evidence and establish findings; cannot silently execute governed actions.

## Malware Analyst / Reverse Engineer

Needs static analysis, sandbox telemetry, disassembly/decompilation, strings, imports, memory views and debugger controls. Produces evidence and findings with provenance.

## Response Manager

Needs action requests, impact previews, policy gates, approval chains, conditions, rollback plans and execution status. Owns or delegates decision authority.

## IT Service Owner

Needs understandable business impact and the ability to approve or challenge actions affecting owned services, subject to policy.

## CISO / Executive

Needs posture, material business impact, exceptional decisions, trends and audit-ready reporting without low-level noise.

## Auditor / Compliance Reviewer

Needs immutable history, policy evidence, decision rationale, approvals, exports and retention controls; normally read-only.

## Platform Administrator

Needs tenant, integration, identity, agent, health and retention controls. Administrative access does not imply investigation or response authority.
