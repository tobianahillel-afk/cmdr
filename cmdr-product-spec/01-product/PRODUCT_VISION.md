# Product Vision

## Objective

CMDR is the operational instrument that lets cyber-defence teams see the situation, establish facts and act with controlled authority without losing context between tools or roles.

## Product promise

CMDR connects the full decision chain:

`signal → operational priority → investigation → evidence → finding → recommendation → governed decision → controlled action → operational outcome`

## Target users

Primary users are SOC analysts, incident responders, DFIR specialists, malware analysts, SOC leads, response managers and security leadership. Secondary users include IT service owners, auditors, legal/compliance stakeholders and platform administrators.

## Differentiation

- Three purposeful consoles instead of one overloaded universal dashboard.
- Evidence-backed transitions between operational, analytical and governance modes.
- Native forensics and reverse-engineering workspaces.
- Controlled response with impact preview, policy gates, approvals, execution, rollback and immutable audit.
- Shared domain objects and context rather than disconnected modules.
- Multi-tenant and MSSP-ready scoping without weakening tenant isolation.

## Success outcomes

- Faster correct prioritisation rather than merely faster alert handling.
- Lower context loss during hand-offs.
- Traceable reasoning from raw event to approved action.
- Reduced unsafe or unauthorised response execution.
- Higher investigation reproducibility and auditability.
